What happens next

  1. You submit this form. Your account is created as pending — nothing is unlocked yet.
  2. Your trainer is emailed. Only the trainer you selected is notified, because they are the one accountable for you.
  3. They approve or decline. You receive an email either way, including a reason if declined.
  4. You sign in. Courses and content unlock only after approval.

Trainers are organised like departments — each group has one dedicated trainer who handles its database. Pick the trainer who owns your group so your request reaches the right person first time.
