What this does and does not do

  • It creates a pending account. They cannot sign in, on any screen, until an administrator approves the application.
  • It grants no role. The role you pick is what they are asking for. An admin may grant something else, and the account holder is emailed which role they actually got.
  • They are emailed. Every admin is notified, and the applicant is told an admin has it.
